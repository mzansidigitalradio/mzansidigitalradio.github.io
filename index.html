About

A South African newsroom for the platforms people already use.

Mzansi Digital Radio publishes short reports in plain language on X and YouTube. We use an AI anchor, and a person decides what goes out. Our aim is simple: tell South Africans what is happening, and be open about how we know.

Plain language

One story, one minute. No jargon, no long explainers you need a degree to follow.

Questions citizens are asking

We follow what South Africans are talking about and report the facts behind it.

Open about AI

Our news is read by an AI anchor, and we say so. Scripts are checked against published sources before they go out.

Our standards

How we handle a story.

Sourced

Every report is checked against published sources or official statements before it goes out, and we name them.

Attributed

What we cannot confirm is labelled as an allegation, or as what people are saying. It is never stated as fact.

Careful with people

We do not name or show private individuals over unconfirmed claims, and we avoid graphic images of victims.

Corrected

When we get something wrong, we fix the post and say what changed. Send corrections to the address below.
